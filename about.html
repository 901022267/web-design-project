About Lesotho

A proud mountain kingdom with a rich culture and remarkable landscapes.

The Kingdom in the Sky

Land of mountains and living traditions

Lesotho is a landlocked country in Southern Africa, surrounded by South Africa. Its high altitude, rugged mountains and valleys make it a distinctive destination for nature lovers and adventure travellers.

The Basotho people have a strong cultural identity expressed through traditional clothing, music, dance, crafts and community life. The Basotho blanket and mokorotlo hat are widely recognised cultural symbols.

Visitors can help protect this heritage by respecting local customs, using local guides and buying crafts directly from local makers.

Responsible tourism

Travel with care

  • Leave natural places as you found them and take rubbish with you.
  • Ask before taking photographs of residents or their homes.
  • Use experienced local guides for remote or high-altitude activities.
  • Respect sacred places, livestock, private land and community rules.
  • Check weather and road conditions, especially in winter.
