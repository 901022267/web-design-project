Featured attraction

Maletsunyane Falls

Maletsunyane Falls is one of Lesotho's best-known natural attractions, located near Semonkong. The water drops into a deep gorge surrounded by rugged highland scenery.

Services and experiences

  • Guided sightseeing and waterfall viewpoints
  • Hiking and nature walks with suitable local guidance
  • Pony trekking in the surrounding highlands
  • Abseiling or other adventure experiences through qualified operators, when offered
  • Local accommodation, meals and cultural experiences in the Semonkong area
  • Photography and scenic excursions
